About FinRadar.ai

FinRadar.ai (“FinRadar,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the www.finradar.ai Platform and provides financial intelligence services. When we collect your personal information through our Platform, we act as the “data controller” – meaning we determine how and why your information is processed.

Personal Information Defined

Throughout this Policy, “personal information” refers to any data that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked to you as an individual. This includes information that qualifies as “personal data,” “personal information,” or “personally identifiable information” under applicable privacy regulations.

How We Collect Information

We obtain personal information through three primary methods:

  1. Direct Collection: Information you voluntarily provide when using our Platform
  2. Automatic Collection: Information gathered automatically through your interactions with our Platform
  3. Third-Party Sources: We may receive information from external parties such as payment processors or service providers

Information Collection and Usage

Account Registration

When creating a FinRadar.ai account, we collect:

  • Full name (first and last name)
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Work nature/professional category
  • Password (stored as a salted cryptographic hash)

For premium subscriptions, payment processing is handled by Stripe, our third-party payment processor. We do not store complete credit card information. Payment data is transmitted directly to Stripe and governed by their privacy policy.

Platform Preferences

We save your customization settings, including chart configurations, display preferences, and analysis tools selections. These settings are stored solely to personalize your experience and are not used for marketing purposes or shared with external parties.

Communication and Support

When you contact our support team or submit inquiries, we collect the information you provide to address your questions and improve our Services. This may include your name, email address, and message content.

Newsletter and Updates

If you subscribe to receive market insights, platform updates, or educational content, we use your email address to deliver this information. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in each email.

Automatically Collected Information

Our Platform automatically gathers certain technical data to optimize performance and user experience:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Pages visited and features accessed
  • Time spent on the Platform
  • Referring website or source
  • Device identifiers and characteristics

Usage Analytics

We analyze how users interact with our Platform to identify popular features, understand user behavior patterns, and guide product improvements. This analysis helps us enhance the financial intelligence tools and market insights we provide.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

FinRadar.ai uses cookies to provide essential Platform functionality. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us maintain your login session and remember your preferences.

Essential Cookies We Use

  • Authentication Cookies: Required to keep you logged in to your account and maintain your session security. These cookies are set when you log in and include “Remember Me” functionality if you select that option.
  • Preference Cookies: Store your customization settings, chart configurations, and display preferences so they persist across sessions.

Optional Cookies We May Use

  • Analytics Cookies: We may use analytics services to understand how users interact with our Platform, which helps us improve our Services and user experience.

Essential cookies are necessary for the Platform to function properly. Disabling them will prevent you from accessing your account and using our Services.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. However, if you disable essential cookies, you will not be able to log in or use FinRadar.ai’s features.

By using our Platform, you acknowledge and consent to the transfer of your information to the United States. We implement appropriate safeguards to protect your information during international transfers and ensure your privacy rights remain protected as described in this Policy.

Data Storage and Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your personal information, including:

Information Retention Periods

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Retention periods are determined based on:

When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it. If immediate deletion is not technically feasible (for example, due to backup archives), we isolate the information from active processing until permanent removal is possible.
